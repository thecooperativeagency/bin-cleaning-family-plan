02 How the DIY machine works

Four systems

  1. Tip / hold — invert or cradle the can over a grate
  2. Wash — gas pressure washer + turbo/wand (spin head later)
  3. Fresh water — 275-gal IBC → filter → washer
  4. Grey water — catch runoff → dirty IBC → RV dump

Stop workflow

  1. Roll can to trailer
  2. Pre-spray degreaser / Fabuloso-type, short dwell
  3. Tip onto grate / cradle
  4. Blast interior, wand lips/edges
  5. Deodorize (OdoBan-type) on handles + interior
  6. Return can; pump hopper → grey when full
  7. Dump grey legally end of route / as needed

Operating rules

  • Density > radius — dominate streets before expanding
  • Monthly > quarterly for cash quality
  • Track churn; aim to keep it low with consistent trash-day routing
  • DIY route day: often ~35–55 stops when dense (speed improves with practice)
  • Commercial (apts/HOA/restaurants) after ~50 residential
  • Grey water legal every single day

08 Financing notes (Double Hot example)

How their teaser works

  • Lender: Geneva Capital (not the trailer company)
  • Soft app: thebintrailer.gogc.com
  • Estimates = 60-month, qualified credit
  • ~$935/mo on $45,999 ≈ ~8% APR, $0 down if strong file
  • Not guaranteed — credit moves APR/payment
  • Startups: may want FICO ~650+, spouse on app if <2 yrs biz
  • Tax + shipping often extra

Ask in writing before deposit

  • Exact term & APR tier
  • Down payment required?
  • Tax + freight inside payment?
  • Total of payments
  • Early payoff penalty?
  • Title / lien holder
  • Build → delivery lead time to our ZIP
  • Warranty: burner, pump, lift, trailer
  • Wet weight + recommended tow vehicle
Family call: do not soft-commit to $935/mo until DIY has proven ~80 homes or there is a hard reason factory is required (e.g. contracted HOA that demands pro gear).
